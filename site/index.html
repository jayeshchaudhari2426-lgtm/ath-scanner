    How signals are decided

    Hammer

    Lower wick at least 2× the body and 60% of the candle's range, upper wick under 15%, range at least 0.8× ATR(14), printed at a fresh 5-bar low. It counts only while price stays above the hammer's low.

    Shooting star

    The mirror image: long upper wick, small lower wick, printed at a fresh 5-bar high. A shooting star within 5% of the prior ATH is treated as a rejection, so the stock is marked AVOID.

    BUY

    • A hammer that has held, on the daily or weekly chart
    • Daily close above the prior ATH (highest high before the last 10 sessions)
    • Breakout volume ≥ 1.5× the 50-day average, close in the top 40% of the candle
    • No more than 5% above the ATH, and stop no more than 10% below entry

    Levels

    • Stop: 0.5% below the hammer low
    • Target: entry + 3 × (entry − stop), a fixed 1:3
    • WAIT: hammer held, price within 10% of ATH, no breakout close yet
    • Quantity: 1 share. Confirmed on both timeframes = High confidence

    Scans the last 10 daily sessions and the last 3 completed weeks, refreshed after each NSE close. Entries are logged at the signal day's close; if a stop and target fall on the same day, the stop is assumed.
